# Promotion and demotion review

Complete this review for one named domain under its current policy. It prepares a recommendation; it does not authorize or apply a scope change.

## Decision rules

1. Every evaluation recommendation requires review by an evaluator independent of the evaluated agent; the evaluated agent cannot serve as its own evaluator. Record the evaluator's identity or role and independence. Unset identity or role, pending review, or unset, unknown, or not-independent status cannot support a positive promotion recommendation.
2. Zero samples, missing evidence, or insufficient coverage stays unknown and means no advancement.
3. Adequate clean evidence that meets the configured criteria supports consideration only; it does not change the active grant.
4. Negative evidence means hold or review a downgrade according to the configured policy.
5. An explicit restriction overrides scores and levels. Check restrictions and host permissions before acting on any grant.
6. Independent evaluation review and recommendation do not approve a scope change or change the active level, tool credentials, or scoring rules. Only the explicitly authorized owner/reviewer or a specifically preapproved policy may approve an actual scope change. The agent may not promote itself or alter its own scoring rules.
7. L10 is still bounded to explicitly delegated routine actions in named domains. Owner-reserved decisions, restrictions, and real host permissions remain in force.
8. A clean evidence window grants no publication, spending, account, or other authority by itself.
